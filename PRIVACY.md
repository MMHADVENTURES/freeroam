# FreeRoam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

**FreeRoam collects no personal data and sends nothing to its developer or to any third party.**

## What FreeRoam stores
Everything stays in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own computer:

- **Your last Street View location**, so the toolbar icon can reopen it
- **Recent places** (up to 8): name or street address, coordinates, heading, and when you visited
- **Settings**: mouse sensitivity, invert-Y, and whether the panel hides during mouse-look

Removing the extension deletes all of it. The **Clear** button in the Recent list empties that list at any time.

## Network requests
FreeRoam runs only on Google Maps (`www.google.com/maps`, `maps.google.com`). The only network requests it makes itself happen when you search for a place by name: it asks Google Maps for the nearest Street View panorama around that place, without sending your Google cookies. Those requests go to Google, the same service you're already using; FreeRoam has no server of its own.

## Permissions
- **storage**: saves the items listed above on your computer
- **Access to Google Maps pages**: adds the control panel and keyboard/mouse controls to Street View

Questions or concerns: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
