# Changelog

## 1.8.1 — Initial public release (2026-09-29)

- WASD + Q/E controls for Google Street View that work as soon as it loads (S is a true step back)
- Pointer-lock mouse-look; Esc releases it without leaving Street View
- Zoom that goes straight in (scroll wheel, pinch, or Z / X) and look speed that stays constant at any zoom
- Docked "Open-World HUD" panel: heading tape, location, place search, recent places with distance, settings; **H** hides it
- Place search lands on the street in front of the place, facing it
- Everything stored locally; no data leaves your computer
